About

I find the broken version of a system more interesting than the working one.

I came into software from electronics engineering, then spent four years at Accenture building and supporting enterprise applications. The part I enjoyed most was troubleshooting: following a problem through the application, the infrastructure, and the database until it finally made sense.

That’s what pulled me toward distributed systems and infrastructure, the layer where failures get interesting. I learn by building: implement it, break it on purpose, then go read the paper.

  • Build firstthen read the paper, then build it again
  • Logs over guessesevidence before theories
  • Break it on purposeor I don’t understand it yet
